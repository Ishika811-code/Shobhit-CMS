## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, dignified, yet modern digital environment tailored for collegiate administration, faculty management, and student engagement. It balances centuries of academic legacy with the agility and clarity expected from contemporary mobile software. 

### Design Personality & Aesthetics
- **Institutional Dignity Meets Contemporary Utility:** The aesthetic marries classical collegiate authority—tradition, intellectual rigor, stability—with crisp, high-efficiency digital ergonomics. Surfaces feel structured, unpolluted, and purposefully structured.
- **Tone & Mood:** Trustworthy, stately, clear, and reassuring. Interactions avoid frivolous bounce or playful excess, preferring crisp, snappy transitions that feel deliberate and official.
- **Stylistic Approach:** Modern Institutional Minimalist. Clean surface tiers, razor-sharp structural alignments, subtle slate border dividers, and focused typographic hierarchy replace decorative noise. Deliberate, high-contrast role badging gives immediate situational awareness across multi-tenant academic dashboards.

## Colors

The palette leverages collegiate heritage tones anchored by dark navy and deep crimson, with academic gold accents and distinct operational status colors.

### Core Swatches & Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2537`):** The institutional bedrock. Used for app bars, primary structural headers, high-emphasis text, active navigation elements, and primary call-to-action buttons.
- **Secondary Crimson (`#8B1E3F`):** The scholastic heritage tint. Used for critical milestone alerts, secondary buttons, grade markers, formal highlights, and accent states.
- **Tertiary Academic Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#B88E18` for accessible text):** Signifies honors, university seals, verified institutional status, achievements, and elevated notices.
- **Neutral Foundation:**
  - Base Ground: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for outer canvas backgrounds to reduce glare.
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` (White) for elevated cards, modal sheets, and inputs.
  - Borders & Outlines: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for structural division; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for accessible form borders.
  - Muted Copy: `#64748B` (Slate 500) for metadata, labels, and secondary supporting copy.

### Role-Specific Indicators
Color semantics denote multi-role contexts across academic workflows:
- **Student Role:** Emerald (`#059669` fill / `#ECFDF5` background / `#047857` border). Represents registration, enrollment, attendance, and student profiles.
- **Faculty Role:** Indigo (`#4338CA` fill / `#EEF2FF` background / `#3730A3` border). Represents course management, grading, research logs, and faculty credentials.
- **Admin Role:** Amber (`#D97706` fill / `#FFFBEB` background / `#B45309` border). Represents institutional oversight, fee approvals, systemic audits, and system configuration.

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all levels, blending contemporary geometric construction with humanistic touches that remain legible in high-density data tables, student lists, and mobile gradebooks.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Use tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with heavy weights (`700`–`800`) to evoke institutional authority without requiring a serif font.
- **Body & Records:** Set standard weights at `400` with open line heights (`1.4`–`1.5`) for readable transcripts, syllabi, and administrative memos.
- **Labels & Badges:** Set at `600` and `700` weights with slight positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.04em`) to ensure instant legibility on dense screen layouts. All role badges and department codes use `label-sm` in full uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

This mobile CMS operates on a fluid 4-column layout for handheld screens, scaling to an 8-column layout for tablets.

### Grid & Canvas Rhythm
- **Mobile Margins:** Standard canvas margin is `1rem` (16px), allowing maximum usable real estate for complex data tables, transcript rows, and schedule matrices.
- **Internal Column Gutter:** Fixed at `1rem` (16px).
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Elements rely on an 8-point base grid:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and labels, chip padding, and indicator tags.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner paddings, stacked list-item gaps, and table cell padding.
  - `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding and field groupings.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Inter-section spacing between academic terms, modules, and feed groupings.
  - `space-xl` (32px): Primary screen-level layout delimiters.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain institutional authority, avoid excessive drop shadows and soft, blurry effects. Visual order relies on subtle ambient depth paired with low-contrast structural borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Hex `#F8FAFC`. All primary content sits over this crisp, cool surface.
- **Resting Cards & Surfaces (Level 1):** Hex `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). A soft shadow provides subtle lift: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Elevated Cards / Quick Actions (Level 2):** Applied when elements are pressed, dragged, or active: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.06), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.04)` with `#CBD5E1` border tone.
- **Floating App Bar, Sticky Filters, & Modal Bottom Sheets (Level 3):** Fixed headers and bottom sheets: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.03)` with top or bottom border demarcation.

## Shapes

The design system uses a moderate corner radius (`roundedness: 2`) to balance mobile usability with academic structure.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Input fields, standard cards, contextual popovers, alert banners, and table containers.
- **Secondary Elements (`0.75rem` - `1rem` / 12px - 16px):** Action sheets, elevated modal drawers, and dashboard modules.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Role badges, status pills, and compact filter chips.
- **Square Sharp (`0px`):** Top app-level navigation bars and bottom navigation docks anchoring directly to screen boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid Navy (`#0F2537`) fill with pure white label (`label-lg`), height `48px`, `8px` border radius. Active state scales to `0.98` with 10% black overlay.
- **Secondary Academic Buttons:** Deep Crimson (`#8B1E3F`) fill for formal institutional triggers (e.g., "Submit Grades", "Enroll Course"), white label.
- **Tertiary / Outline Buttons:** Transparent background with a 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in Navy (`#0F2537`).
- **Gold Accent Buttons:** Solid Academic Gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with Navy text (`#0F2537`) for high-priority calls to action, graduation clearances, and fee processing.

### Chips & Role Badges
- **Role Badges:** Compact layout (`height: 24px`, padding `0 8px`, `label-sm`, uppercase, font weight `700`, fully pill-shaped):
  - **Student:** Emerald text (`#047857`), light emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), 1px emerald border (`#A7F3D0`).
  - **Faculty:** Indigo text (`#3730A3`), light indigo background (`#EEF2FF`), 1px indigo border (`#C7D2FE`).
  - **Admin:** Amber text (`#B45309`), light amber background (`#FFFBEB`), 1px amber border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Filter Chips:** Height `32px`, white surface with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). When active, Navy fill (`#0F2537`) with white text.

### Cards & Institutional Modules
- Crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px `#E2E8F0` borders, `8px` radius, and Level 1 elevation.
- Card headers feature an optional 3px vertical accent bar on the left edge:
  - Gold for university-wide announcements.
  - Crimson for academic deadlines and examination dates.
  - Navy for general administrative notices.

### Form Inputs
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`.
- Text set to `body-md` in Navy (`#0F2537`).
- **Focus State:** 2px border in Navy (`#0F2537`) with a 3px outer glow ring of `#0F2537` at 10% opacity.
- **Error State:** 1.5px border in Crimson (`#8B1E3F`), helper text in Crimson (`#8B1E3F`).

### Lists & Academic Records
- Standardized list items with minimum tap target height of `56px`.
- Divided by 1px solid horizontal lines (`#F1F5F9`).
- Metadata fields use `body-sm` in Slate (`#64748B`), with grade metrics and status codes anchored to the right edge using monospace numeric alignments.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px` with `4px` corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked state uses 1.5px `#94A3B8` border.
- Checked state uses Navy fill (`#0F2537`) with a crisp white tick or inner dot.

### Academic Timetable / Schedule Grid Component
- Clean tabular slots divided into 1-hour intervals with a subtle left indicator colored by course discipline.
- Includes quick-switching tabs for alternating odd/even academic terms and semester calendars.
